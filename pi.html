Principal Investigator

Donghyun Jeon, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor
Department of Biological Science
Kunsan National University

jeon@kunsan.ac.kr

Donghyun Jeon studies genome diversity and complex biological variation through data-intensive approaches. His work combines genome assembly, population-scale genomics, transcriptomics, phenotyping, and quantitative methods to understand biological systems and develop broadly applicable data-science approaches.
