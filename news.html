News and updates

Appointments, publications, and laboratory updates from the Plant Data Science Lab.

September 2026

Plant Data Science Lab opens at Kunsan National University

Donghyun Jeon joins the Department of Biological Science as an Assistant Professor, and PDSL begins its research and teaching activities.

August 2026

Two new plant genome studies are published

Studies on sclareol biosynthesis in Salvia sclarea and the chromosome-scale genome of anise hyssop are published in Industrial Crops and Products and Scientific Data.

July 2026

Sorghum saline–alkaline stress study is published

The co-first-author study “Ion-structured transcriptional network reorganization is associated with tissue-specific adaptation to saline–alkaline stress in sorghum” is published in BMC Plant Biology.

March 2026

Wasabi genome evolution study is published

“Genome stabilization in the neotetraploid wasabi” is published in BMC Plant Biology.

November 2025

Digital breeding review recognized by BRIC Hanbitsa

The review “Digitalizing breeding in plants” is recognized as a highly cited paper by BRIC Hanbitsa.

September 2024

NRF research grant awarded

Donghyun Jeon is selected as principal investigator for the National Research Foundation of Korea’s Research Grant for Ph.D. Students.
