Research

From genomes to decisions.

We connect biological questions with rigorous computation to understand plant diversity and improve breeding.

01

Plant Genomics

Genome assembly, pangenomics, structural variation, and genetic diversity.

02

Genetic Mapping & Association

GWAS, QTL mapping, and the discovery of trait-associated loci.

03

AI-assisted Breeding

Genomic prediction, machine learning, cross design, and progeny selection.

04

Bioinformatics Tools

Reproducible pipelines and computational methods for plant research.
