Research

Genomes and biological variation

BDSL studies how genome diversity contributes to biological traits. The laboratory combines computational genomics with phenotypic, transcriptomic, experimental, and population-scale data.

Research areas

Core research areas

01

Genome evolution

Genome assembly, pangenomics, structural variation, evolutionary genomics, and population genomics.

02

Functional genomics

Transcriptomics, pan-transcriptomics, and integrated analysis of gene expression and genome variation.

03

Data-driven biology

Quantitative genetics, statistical modeling, and AI-based prediction of complex biological traits.

04

Phenomics and bioinformatics

Phenotyping, experimental evaluation, reproducible analysis pipelines, and AI for biological data.

Current projects

Carrot and wheat projects

Carrot

Carrot pan-genome

Deciphering carrot domestication and genome diversity through pan-genome analysis.

Wheat

Wheat population genomics

Identifying genetic variants and candidate genes associated with complex traits in wheat through large-scale population genomics.

Research software

Open and reusable analysis tools

We develop practical bioinformatics tools and reproducible workflows alongside our biological research.

Assembly

OrganelleCleaner

A graph- and sequence-based tool for identifying and removing plastid- and mitochondria-derived contigs from nuclear genome assemblies.

QTL-seq

qtlseq-refine

Python scripts for refining significant ΔSNP-index regions with high-resolution, multi-scale sliding-window analysis and simulation-based validation.
