Research

Plant genomes and agronomic variation

PDSL studies how genome diversity contributes to plant traits and how genomic information can support crop improvement. The laboratory combines computational genomics with phenotypic, transcriptomic, and field data.

Research areas

Core research areas

01

Genome evolution

Genome assembly, pangenomics, structural variation, evolutionary genomics, and population genomics.

02

Functional genomics

Transcriptomics, pan-transcriptomics, and integrated analysis of gene expression and genome variation.

03

Digital breeding

Genomic selection, quantitative genetics, and the prediction of agronomic traits using AI.

04

Phenomics and bioinformatics

Phenotyping, field evaluation, reproducible analysis pipelines, and AI for agricultural and biological data.

Current projects

Carrot and wheat projects

Carrot

Carrot pan-genome

Deciphering carrot domestication and genome diversity through pan-genome analysis.

Wheat

Wheat population genomics

Identifying genetic variants and candidate genes associated with key agronomic traits in wheat through large-scale population genomics.

Research software

Open and reusable analysis tools

We develop practical bioinformatics tools and reproducible workflows alongside our biological research.

Assembly

OrganelleCleaner

A graph- and sequence-based tool for identifying and removing plastid- and mitochondria-derived contigs from nuclear genome assemblies.

QTL-seq

qtlseq-refine

Python scripts for refining significant ΔSNP-index regions with high-resolution, multi-scale sliding-window analysis and simulation-based validation.

View on GitHub →

RNA-seq

Plant RNA-seq pipeline

A reusable bulk RNA-seq workflow for plant transcriptome studies, covering quality control, trimming, alignment, read counting, and differential expression analysis.
